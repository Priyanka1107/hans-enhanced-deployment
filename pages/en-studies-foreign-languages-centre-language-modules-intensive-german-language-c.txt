Intensive German Language Course for refugees from the Ukraine
Funded by the Berlin Senate Department for Science, Health, Care and Equality for refugees from the Ukraine.
The courses are aimed at persons with proof of refugee status in Germany (all countries of origin), persons with Ukrainian citizenship who have fled Ukraine, and international students who have had to flee Ukraine. In addition to proof of refugee status, students must be located in Berlin/Brandenburg, as the courses are held in person.
The B2 course started on 30.10.2023 and ran until 13.02.2024
Are you unable to study in your home country due to the war in the Ukraine?
Have you finished your school education or have already studied a few semesters at university?
Do you already have German language skills at the B1 level and would you like to expand your German language skills to the C1 level in order to prepare for subsequent studies in Germany?
Then this is the perfect course for you!
