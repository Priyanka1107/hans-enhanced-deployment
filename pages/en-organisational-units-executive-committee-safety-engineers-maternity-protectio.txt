Maternity protection
TheMaternity Protection Act (Mutterschutzgesetz, MuSchG)is designed to protect pregnant and nursing mothers and their children
against risks, excessive workloads and detriment to health caused by work and studying
against financial losses and the loss of employment or a study place as a result of pregnancy or giving birth
Inform HTW Berlin about your pregnancy and the anticipated date of birth as soon as possible.
Employeesare required to inform theHR Department.
Studentsare required to fill out the Notification in accordance with §27 Maternity Protection Act [PDF] form and send it by email toSicherheitsingenieure@htw-Berlin.de.
Notification is required in order to protect the employee or student and the unborn child and to determine the period of maternity protection and restricted employment. Appropriate measures will then be instigated to prevent any detriment to health caused by performing certain activities or arising from the employment or study environment.
Risk assessment
As a pregnant or nursing mother, you may be exposed to harmful chemical substances, potentially infectious biological agents and harmful physical factors or work conditions that may constitute a risk for you or your child. TheSafety Engineerstherefore work together with thecampus doctorand the responsible departments to conduct aRisk assessment for pregnant/nursing mothers [PDF]and inform the Berlin Regional Office for Occupational Safety, Health Protection and Technical Safety (LAGetSi).
