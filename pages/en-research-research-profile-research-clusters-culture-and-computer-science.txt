Culture and Computer Science
The "Culture and Computer Science" research cluster focuses on the uses of information and communications technology in the cultural and creative industries. This team of researchers develops and tests new technical and conceptual solutions for the generation, collection, archiving, maintenance, upkeep and presentation of cultural artefacts. An important aspect of the cluster’s work involves preserving important regional industrial heritage and developing its potential for tourism.
