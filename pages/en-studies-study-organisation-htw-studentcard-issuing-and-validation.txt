Validating your HTW StudentCard
Your HTW StudentCard needs to be updated before the start of every semester. Please take your card to acard validation terminal. Once HTW Berlin has received your re-registration fee and if applicable the semester ticket fee, your HTW StudentCard will be printed with details of its validity for the current semester, your study programme code and, if applicable, the semester ticket.
