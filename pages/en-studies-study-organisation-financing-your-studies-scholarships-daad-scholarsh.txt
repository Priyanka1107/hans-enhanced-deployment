Target group
The scholarship is open to applications of international degree seeking students at HTW Berlin with special challenges * during their studies like
special care situations for children
or  relatives in need of care (Caring for 1st and 2nd degree relatives)
level of disability of at least 50% or chronic illness
refugee background
*At least one characteristic must be fulfilled!
Please note:
Only foreign students, i.e. international students who have obtained their higher education entrance qualification abroad, are eligible for funding. (this also includes German schools abroad) or have supplemented their school qualifications acquired abroad with a German preparatory college.
International students who also have German citizenship citizenship are not eligible for STIBET funding.
Selection criteria
The selection is made by an independent committee on the basis of the following criteria:
proven special challenges during studies
Funding
The funding duration and scholarship amount depend on the respectively available funds.
Please note:
Applications will be possible from January 2025.
