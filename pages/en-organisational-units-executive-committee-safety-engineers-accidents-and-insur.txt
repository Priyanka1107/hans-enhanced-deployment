Accident insurance for employees
Obligations
If you are involved in an accident, you are obliged to immediately report any injury or detriment to health to your manager and the Safety Engineer. You must also actively cooperate in the investigation of the accident. If you are not in a condition to do so, this obligation must fulfilled by the first person who first became aware of the incident.
