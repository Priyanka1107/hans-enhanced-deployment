Bio
Stefanie Molthagen-Schnöringstudied German language and literature, communications and applied cultural science at the University of Münster and did her doctorate on the topic of corporate communications and culture. After working as a communications consultant in Hamburg and Berlin, in 2011 she took up a professorship in text and media for the Business Communication Management study programme at HTW Berlin. In April 2014 she was appointed Vice-Dean of the School of Computing, Communication and Business. Since April 2019 she has held the position of Vice-President for Research, Transfer and Science Communications.
