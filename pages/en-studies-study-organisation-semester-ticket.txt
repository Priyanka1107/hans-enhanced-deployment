Semester ticket
Starting in the summer semester 2025, the so called Deutschlandsemesterticket (a fully digital Germany-wide semester ticket, in short: DST) will be issued by S-Bahn Berlin. You have to register online at the S-Bahn Berlin portal to use the DST:=>Click here to go to the online portal of S-Bahn Berlin
Please follow the instructions to download the DST to your smartphone:=>Click here for the instruction manual (PDF-Datei)
You are only eligible to download the DST, if you have paid your semester fee in full.
For detailed information on the DST, please refer to the homepage of the General Student Committee of HTW Berlin (short: AStA):=>Click here to go to the AStA homepage
If you encounter any problems or have questions regarding the order or provision of the DST or the S-Bahn Berlin app, please contact S-Bahn Berlin directly via their contact form:=>Click here for the contact form of S-Bahn Berlin
