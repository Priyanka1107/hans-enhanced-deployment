Catering on campus

Vending machines
Ready meals, some of which are organic, snacks and drinks.
Location: Inner courtyard, House A (atrium)
Using Convini:
Registration: download the Convini app and register.
Shopping: open the store by scanning the barcode in the app and scan the desired products in the store.
Buying: billing takes place automatically via the app. Cash or cards are not required. Payment options in the app: SEPA direct debit, Visa/Mastercard and PayPal.
Enjoying: the products can be removed from the vending machine. Microwaves are available on site for heating frozen products. However, you will need yourown cutlery.
