Terms of use for the Room of Silence
This room is open to all members of the University to relax, meditate or pray.
Please leave your shoes in the anteroom.
Eating, drinking and smoking are not permitted in this room.
The Room of Silence has a deliberately neutral design. Please do not attach any pictures to the walls or leave any objects behind.
The Room of Silence is used by different people. Mutual respect is essential in order to ensure an open space like this can function. A room divider is available so that several people can use the space at the same time.
However, please stay here only as long as necessary so that as many people as possible can use the room for themselves and in peace.
If you notice anydamage to the room or the equipment, please report this to the Technical Services department using thecontact form.
