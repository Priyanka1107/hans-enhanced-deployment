First-aid and emergency equipment
HTW Berlin is well equipped with first-aid and emergency equipment — e.g. first-aid box (bandages and dressings), stretchers, disinfectant dispensers and defibrillators that require no medical training. First-aid boxes are located in all laboratory areas and in all organisational and administrative departments, including the faculty administrations, sports facilities and service units.
