Exemption from Semester Ticket
The semesterticket is your ticket to use the public transportation and is valid all across Germany. The semester ticket is mandatory for all on-campus students at HTW Berlin. Exemptions are possible under special circumstances.
Valid reasons
severely disabled person's pass with token
students who are ill or disabled and therefore cannot use public transport
study-related stay for at least three consecutive months of the respective semester outside the area of validity of theDeutschlandsemesterticket(e.g.internship semester, semester abroad or thesis semester)
enrolment at two universities with the Deutschlandsemesterticket (please also refer to our information ondual enrolment)
Unfortunately, students on ZbwS degree programmes, part-time students and holders of a company ticket or JobTicket can no longer be granted an exemption.
The requirements for exemption from the semester ticket are regulated  in the "Vertrag zum Erwerb des Deutschlandsemestertickets".
For detailed information on reasons for exemption, please contact theAStA(General Student Committee) of HTW: asta@students-htw.de or find information here:www.students-htw.de/update-zum-deutschland-semesterticket/
Application
The request for exemption from mandatory semesterticket fees for the current semester is to be submitted as  original, further proofs (confirmation letters, medical records etc.)  in copies.
You will find the request form inLSF.
Devaluation of the semesterticket
In case of exemption from the semester ticket your ticket will be devaluated and cannot be used anymore for the public tranportation.
