Library card for the HTW Berlin
From winter semester 2023/24, the card will no longer function as a semester ticket. You can find the link to the Deutschlandsemesterticket inLSF.
Your personal StudentCard
Before you start your study programme, a welcome email will be sent to your private email address. The email will contain the login details for yourHTW accountand information on how to obtain your StudentCard.
Please note the following information:
You need to issue your StudentCard yourself using one of the card issuing terminals on theTreskowallee campus, building A, in front of room 149 or on theWilhelminenhof campus, building F, Foyer entrance F1.
After it has been issued, take your StudentCard to a card validation terminal so the current semester and validity period can be printed on it. You will need to validate your card again at the beginning of each semester. Card validation terminals are located onboth campuses of HTW Berlin.Next semester:As soon as you have successfully re-registered for the new semester, you can then validate your card before the start of the new semester. The old and new semester will then be printed on the back of the card.
Replacement issue in case of defect and loss
If your StudentCard is broken or has been lost, you can apply for a new card at theStudent Service Center(SSC). This can be done via theSSC contact formor in person during opening hours. If you are responsible for the defect or the card has been lost, you will have to pay a fee for a replacement card (EC card required).
You will receive a document from the SSC with a new QR code to create a new card. You will also need this document to transfer your Mensa credit. Please present this document at the HTW Mensa cash desk.
