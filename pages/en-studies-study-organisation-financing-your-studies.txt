Financing your studies
At HTW Berlin, only the followingsemester feesare charged to those participating in degree programmes. However, it is wise to plan for the financing of living expenses during degree programmes, becausestudents usually need at least 950 euros per month to support themselves financially.Many are financially supported by their parents during their studies, get apart-time jobor apply for aBAföGstudy grant or ascholarship.
The precise amount of money required to complete a degree programme depends on several cost factors. The largest proportion is usually accounted for by theaccommodation, thus determining the budget available for the remaining expenses.
