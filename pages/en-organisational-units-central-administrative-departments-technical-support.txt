Technical Support
The Technical Support department ensures that the university’s  buildings are maintained and modernised in accordance with their  purpose. The department encompasses numerous different services:
Organisational ServicesThe organisational team encompasses a wide range  of services ranging from post services to central  procurement, event services for internal and external event organisers  and a lost property office. Contact the Head of Department:Katja Schröder
Building Services Management/Building SupervisorThe  Building Services Management department is responsible for building  services, including key management and troubleshooting. Contact the Head  of Department:Frank Klein
Technical ManagementThe  department operates the building services systems of HTW Berlin,  provides media technology and is responsible for energy management.  Contact the Head of Department:Marcus Proske
University ArchiveThe  university archive is where old files are stored when they become due  for archiving. A number of historical documents on the history of HTW  Berlin are also stored here. Contact:Christian Höcky
