PROMOS funding for internships
Through the student mobility scheme PROMOS, the German Academic Exchange  Service (DAAD) seeks to fund a range of activities including  subject-specific work experience in an extra-European context. The  restricted resources of this programme means that applications for  funding from the PROMOS internship programme should be made early.  Please come by the International Office during the opening hours and get more information about the programme.
Table of contents
Who is eligible for funding?
All HTW students who have not previously received PROMOS funding.
What is funded?
This programme is intended to fund internships
Conducted in Asia, Africa or Latin America
With direct subject-specific relevance to the applicant's degree course
With a minimum duration of two months within a calendar year
Conducted in a public or a private company
Depending on the budget internships in North America, New Zealand or Australia may be also funded.
Internships for the winter semester have to start at the latest October 1st to be funded through the PROMOS programme.
Financial support
The students can get a funding between 700 and 1.100 euros depending on the country of destination.
Please note: PROMOS funding is not provided for a traineeship conducted in an international  organization, German embassies or any German diplomatic representation. For this kind of internships does exist special DAAD funding programmes.
The application procedure
Please come first to International Office and get all information about the programme.
Places on the PROMOS programme are awarded on a first-come  first-served basis.
The link for registration/application you will find in the box on the right hand side.
Please inform us about a need of special funding. So we can apply for it.
Notice
Please note that there does not exist any legal claim of getting the PROMOS funding.
