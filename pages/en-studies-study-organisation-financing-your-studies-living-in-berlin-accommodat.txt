Home sweet Home - the HTW Berlin accommodation exchange
Finding an apartment in Berlin is becoming increasingly difficult. The newHTW Berlin accommodation exchange "Home sweet Home"has been introduced to help students to find somewhere to live. The  International Office developed the platform in cooperation with AStA and  the Moodle support team at HTW Berlin.
The idea  is that HTW students, who finish their studies and leave Berlin or go  abroad for one or two semesters, convey or sublet their apartment or  room to other HTW students.
Anyone with a Moodle account can  search for accommodation and post listings for available apartments.
