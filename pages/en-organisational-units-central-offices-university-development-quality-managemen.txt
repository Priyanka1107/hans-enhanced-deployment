(Re-)accreditation of cooperative study programmes (external)
External programme (re)accreditations
As far as cooperative study programmes are concerned, accreditation agencies are usually commissioned to assess quality assurance in teaching and learning. The Central Office for University Development and Quality Management advises and supports cooperative study programmes during the programme (re)accreditation process. Here, the cooperative study programmes are provided with support in terms of
creating self-documentation,
organising on-site audits by experts,
communicating with the accreditation agencies and
formally complying with all criteria related to the procedure.
