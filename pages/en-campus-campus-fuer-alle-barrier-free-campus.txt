Barrier-free campus
Disabled access
HTW Berlin strives to ensure barrier-free access for everyone. Both the Treskowallee campus and the Wilhelminenhof campus offer access for the disabled. This includes:
Dropped kerbs at all sites
Ground-level access or ramps to all buildings
Disabled parking spaces in front of all buildings
Sufficient number of disabled WCs in all buildings
Lifts with voice announcements and Braille or raised buttons for the visually impaired
No barriers or obstacles in buildings
Special guidance system/signage for the disabled
A sufficient number of disabled parking spaces are available at both locations. Disabled students and employees can apply for a free parking permit or a personal parking space subject to certain conditions. For initial enquiries, please contact theDisability Officers of HTW Berlin.
Guidance & support
Disabled and chronically ill students can contact the Disability Officer of HTW Berlin by email, telephone or in person at any time — also before beginning their studies.
The interests of disabled employees are represented by the Disability Representative and the Assistant Disability Representatives.
The University Library provides staff support for disabled students and offers additional support such as extended borrowing periods.
The Information Technology Centre at HTW Berlin offers technical and staff support for disabled students.
The Central Unit Sports at HTW Berlin offers activities for disabled students employees in its sports programme.
