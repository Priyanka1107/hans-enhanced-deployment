Re-registration
Re-registration deadline
You re-register for every semester. No e-mails or payment requests  will be sent to you. If you do not re-register, you will lose your  student status and will be de-registered.
If the final oral exam (colloquium) is the only module that is missing to finish your studies, you should not re-register.
Pay the semester fee:In order to re-register, transfer thesemester feeto the bank account of HTW Berlin. Please note the applicablere-registration deadlines.
Check your re-registration status and retrieve your enrolment certificate:You can check in the LSF portal whether your semester fee has been received and you are officially re-registered. Please note that proper re-registration can only take place if thehealth insurancecontribution has been paid infull.
Validate your student card:As  soon as you are been re-registered for the new semester, you can update  your student card at a card validation terminal. You can also retrieve  enrolment certificates yourself from the LSF portal.
Late re-registration
Even if the regular re-registration deadline has expired, you still have four weeks to re-register. Alate penalty feeis payable for late re-registration and this must be transferred  without any additional request together with your semester fee to the  bank account of HTW Berlin.
Special Cases
If you are
completing yourfinal semesterand only have the final oral examination to do in the coming semester,  you do not need to re-register (exception: Public und  Nonprofit-Management).
taking adual study programmeat two universities and are not exercising your membership rights at  HTW Berlin, you should re-register at the other university and send  proof of re-registration to Students Services.
taking anexternal leave semester, please remember that you still need to re-register.
