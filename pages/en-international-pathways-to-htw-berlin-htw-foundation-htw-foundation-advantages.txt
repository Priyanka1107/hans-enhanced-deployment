HTW Foundation: Advantages
Advantages of attending HTW Berlin as you start your university career
Renommee: With 14,000 students, HTW Berlin is thelargest University of Applied Sciencesin Berlin, whose quality of education is confirmed in rankings regularly and where international students can choose the suitable study programme from more than75 degree programmesin the fields of technology, information technology, business, law, culture, and design.
Internationality: HTW Berlin offers a lively international learning environment with over 3,000 international students from around 120 nations and around 150 partner universities abroad.
Practice-orientation: Teaching at HTW Berlin is practice-oriented and learning groups are small. This facilitates academic success, intensifies contact between students and with lecturers and actively prepares students for their careers.
Counselling and support: Thecomprehensive range of services at HTW Berlinsupports international students in all relevant areas: Career planning, learning strategies, psychological counselling, subject-based supplementary courses, etc.
Leisure time: University sports, student initiatives and the cultural and leisure activities in and around Berlin offer something for everyone's taste.
The HTW Foundation Programme gives prospective students a structured, in-depth introduction to studying in Germany and specifically at HTW Berlin.
