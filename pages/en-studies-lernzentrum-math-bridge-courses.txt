The Learning Center at HTW Berlin offers preparatory math bridge courses for first-year students before the start of the semester.
In these courses, we teach the fundamentals of mathematics to help ease your transition into university studies. Additionally, you’ll have the opportunity to meet fellow students at networking events and build connections.
Math Bridge Courses
The course content is taught in German.
If you require further information, please switch to theGerman versionof the website. There, you will find detailed descriptions of the course structure, schedule, and registration process.
