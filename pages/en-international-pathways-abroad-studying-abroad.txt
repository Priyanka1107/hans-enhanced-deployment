Studying abroad
Spending time abroad at one of our 170 partner universities is a great way to offers a new study experience and  is a great way to get to know to  foreign culture, its people and everyday life. Experience abroad will also expand your horizon and help you to create a strong individual profile.
We will gladly advise you on the many options for studying abroad and provide support with the organisation.
You can read all the information about studying abroadin this presentation, as well as in ourguide, or have it presented to you it in the next informational event.
