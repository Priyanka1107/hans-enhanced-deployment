Application process for minors
Conditions of application
If you are under the age of 18, you are still welcome toapply for a study place at HTW Berlin. The condition is that you have already obtained a higher education entry qualification.
Quota system
Your application will be considered under the quota system if you are
under the age of 18 on the closing date for applications and
resident in Berlin or Brandenburg with a parent or legal guardian.
HTW Berlin will automatically receive information about your age and residency from the details provided in your online application. You do not need to submit an extra application. When you enrol, you will need to provide a residency certificate as proof of your place of residence.The admission quota for applicants under the age of 18 is five percent. Applicants who do not receive a place under the quota system can still be granted admission via the normal application procedure.
