"Stellenticket" HTW Berlin - online job exchange
Students and graduatescan find internships, student assistant positions, final theses, trainee programmes and entry-level positions on our job portal “Stellenticket” HTW Berlin.
Employerscan use “Stellenticket” HTW Berlin to inform students and graduates of HTW Berlin of job and internship vacancies.
