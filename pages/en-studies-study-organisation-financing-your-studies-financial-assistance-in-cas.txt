Table of contents
Financial assistance from Studierendenwerk Berlin
You can apply toStudierendenwerk Berlinfor a bridging loan (Überbrückungsdarlehen), a study loan (Studienbegleitdarlehen) or support from the hardship fund (Notfonds).
Hardship funds of the Evangelische Studierendengemeinde Berlin
If you are from Africa, Asia (with the exception of South Korea), Latin America or eastern Europe and are in financial need, you may be eligible for support from thehardship funds of the Evangelische Studierendengemeinde Berlin(Berlin Evangelical student community). To qualify you must be able to show commitment in social or developmental areas.
Semester ticket subsidy
In particular hardship cases, e.g. during pregnancy or limited permission to work, you can apply for asemester ticket subsidy.
DAAD scholarship for more equal opportunities
Thisscholarshipis open to applications of international degree seeking students at HTW Berlin with special challenges during their studies.
