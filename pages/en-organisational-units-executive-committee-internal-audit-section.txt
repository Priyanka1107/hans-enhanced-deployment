Internal Audit Section
The Internal Audit Section is the internal auditing body of HTW Berlin. Its purpose is to perform internal audits with the goal of ensuring that the university's operations are managed economically and with due diligence. The Internal Audit Section also acts in a consulting capacity, providing advisory services on request and advising the University Board as required. In addition, it can also perform preliminary assessments of planned projects, in particular with regard to financial viability and occupational health and safety.
