Complaints Office for employees in accordance with the German General Equal Treatment Act (AGG)
You can contact the Complaints Office if you have been discriminated against in connection with your employment relationship on the basis of one of the characteristics listed in the German General Equal Treatment Act (AGG) (characteristics: gender incl. gender identity, ethnic origin or racial ascription, religion or belief, disability, age, sexual orientation).
