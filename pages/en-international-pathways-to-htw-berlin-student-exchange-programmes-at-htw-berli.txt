Additional Course Enrollment Information
Some of our study programmes have specific requirements that need to be considered when preparing your Learning Agreement and enrolling in courses. Please check if your study programme is listed below for additional information. Contact the person responsible for the study programme should any questions remain unclear.
Department 3
International Business (Bachelor) – HORIZON
When enrolling in courses of this study programme please take the following into consideration:
no more than 30 ECTS in total
please specify alternative courses on your Learning Agreement "before the mobility", in case enrollment in your priority course should not be possible.
Please ONLY choose fromthese courses.
Department 5
Fashion Design
Coordinator:Prof. Dr. Andrea Bookhagen
When enrolling in courses of this study programme please take the following into consideration:
only one collection project
courses per semester must not exceed 30 ECTS in total
when choosing course modules, please do so in accordance with your previous experience and acquired skills.
courses for the semester equivalent to your own at your home university are to be preferred – courses in higher semesters have higher thematic and technical prerequisites
if possible, only choose courses from one single semester to avoid scheduling conflicts
Please note that the study programme Fashion Design only offers lectures taught in German. However, there will be individual support in English so that applicants are not required to have German language skills.
