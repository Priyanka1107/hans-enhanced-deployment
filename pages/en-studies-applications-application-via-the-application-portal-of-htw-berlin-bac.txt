Important: Upload your documents in full and in time
During your online application you will be informed which documents you need to upload. Based on the information you provided, the system will ask forrequired documents.If any requested document is unclear, please contact theStudent Service Centrein plenty of time before the application deadline.
The documents must be received by HTW Berlin before the application deadline. The date of receipt at HTW Berlin is decisive. If your application documents are not complete by the application deadline, your application will be excluded from the admissions procedure.
Pleasedo not upload documents that have not been requested.
Send all required documents asofficially attested copies. Information on how to obtain officially attested copies of documents from abroad is available from the Federal Foreign Office.
All application documents must be uploadedinGerman or English. Documents in other languages require an officially attested German or English translation. A list of officially authorised translators in Germany can be obtained from the BDÜ (Federal Association of Interpreters and Translators).
