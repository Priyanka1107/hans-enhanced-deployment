German Scholarship
The Deutschlandstipendium (German scholarship) offers a win-win situation for all participants. It enables students to concentrate better on their studies by providing financial support while offering companies and private individuals the chance to support qualified and committed young people and to potentially recruit them as valuable resources.
The German Scholarship is a cooperation partnership between the funding bodies and the Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF). The BMBF will finance the second half of the funding if the first half is provided by companies or private individuals. By offering a German Scholarship, companies can make a significant contribution to the educational landscape and promotion of young talent in Germany.
