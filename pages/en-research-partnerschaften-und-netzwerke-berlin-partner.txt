Berlin-Partner Network
HTW Berlin is actively involved in shapingBerlin as a science location. It organises events, participates in campaigns and cooperates with partners in the field of research. Acting as ambassadors, members of the university promote Berlin’s reputation as an exciting and multifaceted science location.
Enterprise Europe Network Berlin-Brandenburg
As part of the Berlin-Partner Network, HTW Berlin has access to theEnterprise Europe Network Berlin-Brandenburg. At the local level, the network provides practical and largely free support with organising business, technology and project partnerships in Europe and beyond. For example, EEN supports companies in areas relating to innovation and internationalisation.
