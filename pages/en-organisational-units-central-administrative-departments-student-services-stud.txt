Student Service Centre
Office hours
You are welcome to visit the Student Service Center on the Treskowallee campus, Building A, Room 040 during our office hours without making an appointment. The mailbox in front of the SSC is emptied on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
We'll be there for you!
For your requests, you are welcome to use our office hours on campus Treskowallee (Tuesday and Thursday). Our hotline is available on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Inquiries can also be sent via our contact form at any time and will be forwarded to the responsible departement.
We make every effort to communicate in a gender-neutral manner. Therefore, you are welcome to inform us in your request if you wish to be formally addressed in a certain way.
