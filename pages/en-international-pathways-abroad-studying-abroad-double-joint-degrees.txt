Double & Joint Degrees
In addition to the variety of student exchange opportunities at one of HTW Berlin's around 150 partner universities, students of certain study programs are also able to take part in special Double Degree programs.
Detailled information on the various Double Degree programs can be obtained on the website of the responsible faculty:
Additional information
Double Degree agreements are conceived in a way that enables students  inegrated and coordinated studies both at their home as well as at  their host university, without an unnnecessary extension of their period  of study.
Thus, students are provided with the opportunity to gain valuable  international experience, improve their intercultural and linguistic  skills and broaden their horizon by obtaining unique perspectives within  both partner countries.
All aforementioned aspects significantly increase the employability  of Double Degree graduates and prepare them for the diverse demands of  the modern working world in the most optimal way.
