Teaching and studying are organised in the fivefaculties of HTW Berlin. You can contact your faculty administration, e.g. if you have problems with your lecture schedule or want to register your thesis.
Eachstudy programmehas its own website. This is where you will find the Study and Examination Regulations that apply to your study programme, an overview of all modules, regulations regarding internships and the final thesis as well as contact details for all lecturers.
TheHTW StudentCardfunctions as a student ID card, semester ticket and library card. It must be re-activated every semester.
Do you have a question about your studies or would you like some career advice? Would you like to use the WiFi on campus or look for research literature in the library? If so, then take advantage of theservice and counsellingprovided by HTW Berlin.
TheSustainability Certificateis a programme at HTW Berlin that gives students the opportunity to take an in-depth look at sustainable development.
