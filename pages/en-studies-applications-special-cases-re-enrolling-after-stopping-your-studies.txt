Re-enrolling after stopping your studies
Do you wish to continue the study programme which you started at HTW Berlin but decided to stop for whatever reason?If so, send are-enrolment requestto theStudent Services Departmentwithin the regularapplication deadlines. In this signed request, please provide your personal details including your current address, email address and information about the study programme. You also need to confirm that you have not been enrolled at any other university during your absence from HTW Berlin.
