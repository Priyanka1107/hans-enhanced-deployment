Printing certificates from the LSF
You can print out certificates yourself in theLSF campus management systemunder the menu item "Bescheinigungen" (certificates), e.g.
verified enrolment certificates in German and English
verified module certificates in German and English
certificate of extension of the standard study period due to COVID19 restrictions in German and English
Verified certificates
The certificates that you print out from the LSF campus management system always include a verification symbol. This symbol allows every institution (family allowance/child benefit office, German state student grant and loan agency (BAföG) etc.) to check that
the certificate was issued by HTW Berlin and
the information is accurate.
