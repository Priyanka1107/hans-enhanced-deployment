Legal Section
The Legal Section represents the university with regard to legal issues, particularly in connection with higher education law, the University Board, the faculties and the University Administration. It supports the faculties and University Administration in the process of drafting contracts and cooperation agreements. It assists with the development and legal assessment of the University Regulations and Articles of HTW Berlin that are published in the Official Information Circulars. The Legal Section is also involved in legal proceedings relating to administrative, civil and social law.
