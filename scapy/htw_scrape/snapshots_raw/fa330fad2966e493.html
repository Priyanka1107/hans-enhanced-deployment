Curriculum Innovation Hub

Reliable framework conditions are created for a future-oriented blended curriculum with the aim of establishing blended curricula as a new university norm. Further information can be found on the Project page.

Future Professors Programme

As part of the Talent Identification & Empowerment (TIEs) project, a candidate academy for young academic professionals will be set up at the TSC. It will support interested candidates on their path to a professorship through training, a coaching program and support during the appointment process.

Patricia Heitmann

Project Coordinator Future Professors Programme

Nadja Bajerski

Program developer for further education and training

EU on AIR – European University on AI in Curricula, Smart UniverCity and (Return) Mobility

HTW Berlin is the only university of applied sciences in Berlin that has been a member of EUonAIR since January 2025—a European alliance of ten technical and business-oriented universities that aim to anchor artificial intelligence in education, research, and society in an ethically responsible and innovative manner. The TSC supports the implementation of the project with its comprehensive expertise in the areas of didactics and media production. Further information is available on the project page.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
