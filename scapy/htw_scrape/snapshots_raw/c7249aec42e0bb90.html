Teaching Support Centre

The Teaching Service Center, or LSC for short, supports you in making your teaching forward-looking, inspiring, and effective. We offer individual consulting and media production, topic-specific workshops or training courses, and open doors to people, projects, and inspiration within the university.

Whether it's AI integration, motivating learning settings, or tailor-made exam concepts: designing good learning processes is a challenging task, and we are here to support you with advice, assistance, and tools so that you can focus entirely on your students.

Good teaching has enormous social value. It requires expertise, inspiration, and continuous development. The LSC is there to provide you with practical, solution-oriented support—and to recognize and highlight your work. That's why we are more than just a service point.

Overview of our services

  • Teaching advice: The LSC team finds individual solutions to your questions.
  • Media production: Support with brainstorming, concept development and production
  • Further training for teachers: We offer a wide range of opportunities for your further education and networking through regular events 
  • "Tag der Lehre" (Teaching Day): Symposium on current topics in university teaching
  • Teaching Innovation Fund: Support programme for funded teaching projects
  • Teaching innovations - LEHRGUT initiative: Inspiring stories from the world of teaching
  • Interim feedback – Teaching Analysis Poll: Qualitative method for student feedback during the semester
  • Teaching observation: Developing personal teaching skills

Contact

You can reach us at lehre@htw-berlin.de. If you would like to visit us in person, you can find us at the Wilhelminenhof campus in room WH B 004. We look forward to hearing from you!

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
