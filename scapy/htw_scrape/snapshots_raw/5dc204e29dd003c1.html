BVG public transport timetable information

Bikesharing or on foot

You only need a few minutes by bike from S Schöneweide. You can hire bikes from nextbike (bike sharing) at Schöneweide S-Bahn station and return them on the campus. 

From Schöneweide S-Bahn station, you can reach the campus on foot via the ‘Kaisersteg’ in approx. 20-25 minutes.

 

 

 

Opening hours

  • Monday to Friday: 6.30 a.m. to 10.00 p.m.
  • Saturday: 6.30 a.m. to 6.00 p.m. (with access card)

Between Christmas and New Year all HTW Berlin buildings are closed. Different opening hours apply for individual organisational units.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
