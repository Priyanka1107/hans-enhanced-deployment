Scholarships

A scholarship can offer more than just financial relief. It provides you with a support network, opportunities for professional development and personal advancement. Your social commitment, special life circumstances and your career to date are important factors in the selection process. Good grades are an advantage.

Scholarship databases

  • mystipendium.de — individual matching with over 3,000 scholarship programmes
  • e-fellows.net — over 1,000 scholarships for different stages of life and fields of study
  • Stipendium Plus — association of nationwide organisations for the promotion of gifted students
  • DAAD scholarship database — for international students and financial support abroad
  • Arbeiterkind.de — for all those who are the first in their family to study
  • ApplicAid.org — for educational achievers

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
