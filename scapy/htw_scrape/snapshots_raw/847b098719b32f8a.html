HTW Berlin is committed to equal opportunities:

  • Applications from women and people with migration history are especially welcome.
  • Preference will be given to severely disabled people if they have the same aptitude. 

The HTW Berlin has a lot to offer!

  • Mobile working
  • Flexible working hours
  • ttractive continuing education and sports opportunities
  • Health management
  • Diverse and varied range of tasks
  • Company pension scheme
  • 30 days holiday per year (plus Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve)
  • Payment according to TVöD/VKA

Climate protection during business trips

"HTW Berlin takes climate protection seriously. We were the first university in Germany to decide that from 2020 we would no longer accept air travel for any business trips that can be made within six hours by train. We also ensure compensation for all emissions caused by air travel. In keeping with our climate protection policy, we would therefore be very pleased if our applicants would also travel by train for an interview if this is feasible in a reasonable amount of time."

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
