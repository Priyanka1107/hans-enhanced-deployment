Retreats

Children's and family rooms

What for?
Self-organised childcare (playing, resting, breastfeeding)

Where?

WH C 622 TA E
Information
  • Rules for using the children's and family room [PDF] - in German
  • Key against deposit at the porter
  • Children may only stay in the children's room under supervision
  • For reasons of hygiene, please bring your own blanket if your child is to sleep in the children's room.

Medical rest rooms

What for?

  • Resting for acute health complaints, e.g. migraines, headaches, circulatory problems
  • Retreat and relaxation area for pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers
  • First aid treatment for accidents
  • Medical treatments, vaccinations, examinations by the company doctor

Where?

Wilhelminenhof Campus

WH A 401 WH C 272 WH G 113

Treskowallee Campus

TA C 117 (Central relaxation room) TA C 116 (emergency medical care only)

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
