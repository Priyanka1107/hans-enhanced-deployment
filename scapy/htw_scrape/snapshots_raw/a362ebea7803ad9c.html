Partner universities

The HTW Berlin co-operates with around 160 partner universities around the world. 100 of these institutions work within the EU Erasmus programme. We also co-operate with a further 60 institutions of higher education outside the EU and across the world.

A detailed overview of our partnerships can be found on Mobility Online.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
