System accreditation

HTW Berlin is the first university in Berlin to receive the quality seal from the Accreditation Council. System accreditation status means that HTW Berlin is able to guarantee the quality standards of its study programmes on the basis of its quality management system.

European Digital Woman Award

HTW Berlin is the first "Digital Impact Organisation of the Year". The award was presented as part of the European Digital Woman Award 2013. HTW Berlin was praised for its innovative efforts to promote the digital skills of girls and women during their studies and professional careers.

Special integration award of the State of Berlin / "Barrier-free Berlin" seal

HTW Berlin strives to promote the integration of people with handicaps. The award was presented for the disabled-friendly access of both campus locations and its close cooperation with the Lichtenberg workshop for the disabled and the Stephanus workshops.

University of High Performance Sports

HTW Berlin is an official "Partner University of High Performance Sports” in association with the HU, TU, FU, Charité, HWR, Beuth Hochschule and the H:G University for Health & Sports, Technology & Arts (H:G). The partner universities promote the dual careers of student athletes in an exemplary manner.

Barrier-free Berlin

In 2007 the Treskowallee Campus was awarded the "Barrier-free Berlin" quality seal in recognition of its unlimited accessibility for the disabled. One year later the Wilhelminenhof Campus also received the quality seal.

exzellent: kooperation

The Stephanus sheltered workshops and HTW Berlin were delighted to win the "Best Cooperation" category in 2012. The Federal Association of Workshops for Adapted Work presented the award in recognition of the WaschBAR concept, which combines catering and a service centre on the Wilhelminenhof Campus.

"Premium D-A-CH" alumni award

The Career Services department received the "Premium D-A-CH" alumni award in 2012. The jury was impressed by the department’s close links with companies in the region and its combination of alumni management and career services.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
