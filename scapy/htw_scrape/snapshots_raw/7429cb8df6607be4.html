Student Service Centre

The Student Service Centre is the central point of contact for applicants, students and alumni. Here, questions and concerns are answered directly or forwarded to the appropriate departments.

Our responsibilities in detail
  • Providing information and coordinating and forwarding enquiries and applications
  • Processing de-registrations
  • Replacement HTW Student Card
  • Issuing replacement documents, e.g. university semester, enrolment and de-registration certificates
  • Certification of documents
  • Clarification of questions regarding the Germany semester ticket

Contact form

The best way to contact us is via our contact form. 

Please note: For certificates and authentications, please use this form exclusively.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
