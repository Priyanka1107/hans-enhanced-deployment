BIP "Practical Oriented Systemic Design" in Nantes

In October 2022 after an online learning phase MA System Design students traveled to Nantes with Prof. Pelin Celik, Prof. Sebastian Feucht and Olivia Hidalgo Miranda. In cooperation with automobile manufacturer Stellantis students and teachers from HTW, l'École de Design Nantes Atlantique and Oslo Metropolitan University collaborated to tackle the subject of mobility.

BIP "Advanced Topics in Asset Management" in Vienna

Students of MA Finanzmathematik visit the University of Applied Sciences BFI Vienna in March 2023 with Prof. Dietmar Hillebrand. Students and teachers from the University of Bologna, the University of Maribor, the University of Economics in Katowice, and the Alexandru Ioan Cuza University of Iași also take part in the collaborative format.

Upcoming COIL "Law Game 2023"

As in previous years, students of LLB Wirtschaftsrecht (Commercial Law) will participate with Prof. Michael Janesch in the Businet Law Game, where they solve jurisprudential tasks in online teams with members from various European universities. They also travel to Helsinki for Law Week at Metropolia University.

Virtual Exchange in International Teaching

HTW Berlin supports teachers and students wishing to organise virtual exchange and virtual learning projects in cooperation with international partner institutions. The term "Virtual Exchange" comprises a range of different formats, where international students collaborate in a virtual setting and with the help of digital tools on specific assignments. Examples of such formats include Virtual Global Classroom, Collaborative Online International Learning (COIL), and Blended Intensive Programmes (BIP).

Objectives of Virtual Exchange

  • to establish new formats of digital learning and teaching, to expand those already at hand, and to strengthen international networks
  • to improve the possibilities for non-mobile students to participate in international exchange and to promote intercultural sensitivities among all participants
  • further development of digital and methodological skills in online learning in an international context
  • sustainable establishment of our academic strategy „Internationalisation at Home“ and advancement of our local education program
