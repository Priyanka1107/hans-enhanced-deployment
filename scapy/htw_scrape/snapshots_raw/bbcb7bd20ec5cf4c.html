HTW Foundation: Preparation for your Studies in Germany

Are you coming from abroad and want to study in Germany? But you don't yet meet all the language requirements for studying in Germany? Then the fee-based HTW Foundation courses offer you the perfect preparation for your Bachelor’s or Master’s degree — including personal advice and support on choosing a degree program, applying, and preparing for your studies.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
