Master

If you are applying for a degree programme in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, Health, Energy & Environment, Law or Construction & Real Estate Management, please proceed as follows.

List of degree programmes I

At HTW Berlin, this applies to the following Master’s degree programmes:

  • Angewandte Informatik
  • Arbeits- und Personalmanagement
  • Bauingenieurwesen
  • Betriebliche Umweltinformatik
  • Computer Engineering
  • Construction and Real Estate Management
  • Elektrotechnik
  • Facility Management
  • Fahrzeugtechnik
  • Finance, Accounting, Corporate Law, Taxation
  • Finanzmathematik, Aktuar- und Risikomanagement
  • Gebäudeenergie- und -informationstechnik
  • Industrial Sales and Innovation Management
  • Informatik in Ingenieurwissenschaften
  • Informations- und Kommunikationstechnik
  • International Business
  • Internationaler Studiengang Medieninformatik
  • Life Science Engineering
  • Maschinenbau
  • Mikrosystemtechnik
  • Regenerative Energien
  • Unternehmenssteuerrecht
  • Wirtschaftsinformatik
  • Wirtschaftsingenieurwesen
  • Wirtschaftskommunikation
  • Wirtschaftsrecht

You should apply via the HTW application portal if one of the following requirements applies to you:

  • You obtained your first academic degree in Germany.
  • You are a national of an EU or EEA country.
✗ HTW application portal (15.11. - 15.12.22)

The following application periods apply:

  • Winter semester: 16.05. - 15.06.
  • Sommersemester: 15.11. - 15.12.

or

You should apply via uni-assist if you

  • obtained your first academic degree outside Germany and
  • are not a national of an EU or EEA country.
✗ uni-assist (01.10.22 - 15.12.23)

The following application periods apply:

  • Winter semester: 02.04. - 15.06.
  • Summer semester: 01.10. - 15.12.

  • Master’s application via the HTW application portal

Your preferred degree programme is not subject to the NC requirement.

  • Betriebliche Umweltinformatik

You are applying for a design- or culture-related degree programme.
At HTW Berlin, this applies to the following Master’s degree programmes:
  • Bekleidungstechnik/Konfektion
  • Konservierung und Restaurierung (with aptitude test)
  • Modedesign (with aptitude test)
  • Museumsmanagement und -kommunikation
  • Public Design (with aptitude test)
  • System Design/Game Design (with aptitude test)

  • Professional IT-Business
  • Real Estate Management

  • European Economic Policy

  • Winter semester: 15.05. - 15.06.
  • Summer semester: 15.11. - 15.12.

Application periods for further education Master’s programmes:

  • Winter semester: 15.05. - 15.07.
  • Summer semester: 15.11. - 15.01.

  • Summer semester: 10.11. -15.12.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
