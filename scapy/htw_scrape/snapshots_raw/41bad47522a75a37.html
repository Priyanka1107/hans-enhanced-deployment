Counselling service at Studierendenwerk Berlin

The social counselling service of the StudierendenWERK Berlin offers advice and support in matters of student finance and accommodation. Consultation times and events are listed in the events calendar.

Accommodation

We have compiled tips on finding accommodation and information on halls of residence, shared accommodation, rental costs and support options for you.

Scholarships

This section contains an overview of scholarship programmes and a list of liaison lecturers at HTW Berlin.

Working alongside your studies

  • The Career Services at HTW Berlin help you with your job search.
  • Jobangebote can be found on the HTW Berlin job vacancies page (“Stellenticket”), and job vacancies  advertisements for student assistants are posted on the university website.
  • Information on part-time jobs from the Studierendenwerk Berlin and, for international students specifically, from the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)

Educational support allowance (BAföG)

  • BAföG is the most important state student grant, the level of which depends on the income of parents and students.
  • Application advice and submission takes place via the Studierendenwerk Berlin.

Student loans

  • Student loans can be applied for during advanced studies and must be repaid.
  • It is advisable to check your financial situation carefully before taking out a loan and apply for a social counselling session from the Studierendenwerk Berlin.

Help in financial emergencies

Students in acute financial crisis can receive support from the StudierendenWERK Berlin or from social counselling centres.

  • Study start-up grant
  • Graduation grant for successful completion of your studies
  • Hardship fund of the Berlin Evangelical student community (in German)
  • Semester ticket subsidy (in German)

Average monthly expenditure

Accommodation (rent + ancillary costs)(depending on location and type of accommodation)
Food250 EUR
Clothing30 - 80 EUR
Telephone, internet50 EUR
Learning materials20 - 100 EUR (depending on the degree programme)
Health insurance0 - 220 EUR (depending on insurance status)
Leisure, culture, sport50 - 100 EUR
Totalbetween 700 - 1,600 EUR

In addition, per year:

  • 2x semester fee incl. semester ticket
  • Other costs, e.g. liability insurance

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
