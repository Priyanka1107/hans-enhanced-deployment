University

  • Founded in 1994
  • 14,500 students, largest university of applied sciences in Berlin
  • 80 study programmes in the areas of design, computing, culture, technology and business
  • Key research areas: digital technologies for industry and society, technologies for health and life, renewable energies and resource efficiency, design and culture, sustainable management, entrepreneurship and the future of work
  • 160 partner universities in Europe and overseas

For further information see the university profile.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
