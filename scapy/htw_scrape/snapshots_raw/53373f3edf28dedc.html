UNIcert® II

  • Requirements:
    • Regular attendance in all courses (at least 80%)
    • Achievement of certificate levels M2 and M3
  • Scope: 8 weekly study hours
  • Examination: Cumulative model
  • Grading: The final grade is calculated from the average of all individual grades, which are equally weighted

Certificates

At the Foreign Languages Centre you can prepare for the internationally recognised English certificates TOEFL (no course in WS 25/26) and TOEIC L&R. All preparatory courses — regardless of the certificate examination — culminate with a university-internal module certificate. This module certificate can help you when applying to the International Office for a semester abroad in an English-speaking country.

TOEIC L&R

  • Target group: Primarily students wishing to study abroad in an English-speaking country
  • Scope: 1× 2 weekly study hours (34 hours)
  • Examination: The examination can be taken 2× year at HTW Berlin.

TOEFL (no course in WS 25/26)

  • Target group: Students wishing to study in the USA, Canada or Japan
  • Scope: 1× 4 weekly study hours or 68 intensive hours
  • Examination: Only a preparatory course can be taken at HTW Berlin. The examination is subject to fees and can only be taken at authorised institutions. During the course students acquire the skills and abilities required for the examination.
