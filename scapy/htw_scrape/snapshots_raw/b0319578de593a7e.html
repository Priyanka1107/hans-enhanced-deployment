Important documents for your studies

Study organisation

  • Module registration: Course enrolment takes place in several phases. There are special regulations for certain groups (e.g. students with children).
  • Examination periods: There are two examination periods per semester, which are announced via the academic calendar.
  • Examination registration: Examinations must be registered. Please note the deadlines and regulations of your examination regulations.
  • Compulsory internship: There are special formalities and registration processes for the compulsory internship in the Bachelor's programme.
  • Foreign languages: Bachelor's students must take at least one foreign language and choose an examination option.

Service & Advice

  • Student Service Centre: First point of contact for questions regarding enrolment, re-registration, leave of absence, examinations, certificates and de-registration.
  • Departmental Administration: Contact for problems with the curriculum, registering your thesis or other study-related questions.
  • Student Advisory & Career Services: Support with all questions relating to your studies, including career planning.
  • International Office: Opportunity to study abroad for up to two semesters. International students are also welcome.
  • FAQ for students: Answers to the most frequently asked questions about studying, examinations, internships, re-registration and semester fees.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
