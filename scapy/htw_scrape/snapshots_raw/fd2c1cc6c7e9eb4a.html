FAQ

FAQ for students

How do I enrol on my courses in the first semester?

Full-time study programmes: you are automatically registered for almost all courses in the first semester according to the sample timetable. You only have to enrol on the courses for the supplementary and foreign language modules independently. Exception: Communication Design.

Part-time study programmes: create your own timetable. If you have any questions, please contact the faculty administration or the programme counselling service.

What access data do I need for course enrolment?

You use your HTW Berlin login (user name and password) that you received when you enrolled.

Why can't I enrol on a course in the LSF campus management system?

Reasons may include: 

  • incorrect re-registration (semester fee not paid on time, unpaid late fee)
  • restriction on re-registration due to missing health insurance notification
  • no enrolment phase.
Do I enrol on all courses via the LSF?

Not always. Some courses are enrolled on outside the LSF. Information on this can be found in the respective LSF course description under “Notes on enrolment”.

Do I have to register for each module course individually?

You can only register for one course per module. If you are admitted to this course, you will automatically be admitted to all other courses in the module. The LSF shows exactly which courses you can register for and which courses you are automatically enrolled on. If there are several groups with different times scheduled in a course, you can select two groups and determine your preference for one of these. However, you only get one place — in one of the two groups.

How does prioritisation work if multiple times are scheduled?

Enter your preferred time as your first priority. If there is no space remaining in this group, the second priority is selected automatically. Without choosing a second priority, your chances of being allocated a place are reduced.

What principle is used to allocate places?

The allocation is made automatically according to an internal ranking — regardless of the time and date on which you register your enrolment requests in LSF. Special regulations exist for certain groups of students.

Are there enrolment deadlines?

Yes. Course enrolment runs in several phases. The exact dates can be found in the LSF portal.

Can I change my enrolment at a later date?

You can adjust your enrolment at any time during phase 1 and the remaining place allocation phase, but not afterwards. The date/time you do this within the phase does not affect the allocation of places. There is no guarantee of a place. Therefore, please pay attention to the enrolment figures in the LSF.

Where can I find an overview of compulsory and elective courses?

In the curriculum of your degree programme, which is included in your specific study regulations. Elective modules that are not offered every semester also exist.

How do I find out about changes made by lecturers?

You will be notified of any changes by email to your HTW Berlin email address.

The register/de-register button is not active – why?

The enrolment system is only accessible during the unlocked phases. 

Where can I get help?
  • Instructions
  • Organisational issues: faculty administration, for language modules Foreign Languages Centre
  • Technical problems: IT help centre

FAQ for lecturers and teaching staff

How do I register in the LSF campus management portal?

Please use the access data you received from the ITC (Information Technology Centre). Employees of HTW Berlin usually already have an HTW Berlin account. Lecturers can apply for access via their respective faculties.

What happens during the online enrolment process?

Students submit their enrolment requests for courses online. Places are allocated automatically according to defined criteria and available capacity.

How are places allocated in my courses?

The allocation procedure is explained under Course enrolment. Please note the information provided by your faculty on organisational issues.

Can the number of places on my courses be adjusted?

The maximum number of participants depends on curricular requirements, room capacities and current demand. You can see the upper limit set for your course in the LSF under “Enrolment figures”.

How is course enrolment related to examination registration?

In exceptional cases, re-enrolment may be necessary. This is regulated by the respective study and examination regulations of your degree programme.

Can I contact my participants by email?

Yes, you can use the LSF campus management system to send an email to all students on the enrolment list — without having to know their individual email addresses. Please remind your students to check their HTW Berlin email address regularly.

Can I also send attachments with the LSF mail function?

No, the LSF mail function does not permit the sending of attachments. To exchange materials, please use the e-learning platforms or other services offered by your faculty. Your faculty will be happy to inform you of the available options.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
