International Winter and Summer Schools

Scholarship for Summer programm in the USA 2026

Fulbright Germany is offering bachelor's students at universities a fully funded study program in the USA in summer 2026. The program focuses on the topic of sustainability. The title is: A Culture of Sustainability. 

  • Dates: August 2 to 28, 2026
  • Location: North Carolina State University, Raleigh, North Carolina, USA
  • Courses: "The program is dedicated to the interdisciplinary and intercultural examination of the topic of sustainability. Courses cover the topics of sustainable development, intercultural skills, and social justice. Cultural and topic-related excursions in Raleigh's Research Triangle enrich the program.”
  • Costs: Scholarship, fully funded (including flight, accomodation, and meals on campus)
  • Application deadline: Januar 22, 2026, 1:59 p.m. CET

Please note: Students must be enrolled in a full-time program and have German citizenship. 

Contact: If you have any questions, please contact Thomas Mutzke at Fulbright Germany: usa-seminar@fulbright.de

All information at a glance is available online: A Culture of Sustainability. 

Please use the hashtag #FulbrightGermany for the announcement on LinkedIn, Facebook und Instagram.

Good luck! 

AI in Business in Helsinki in June 2026

Students of HTW Berlin are invited to join the Haaga-Helia Joint Summer School 2026, co-organized with the University of Ljubljana, School of Economics and Business. This two-week program, held from 1st to 12th June 2026, offers a unique opportunity to explore Artificial Intelligence in Business through hands-on learning, international collaboration, and cultural immersion in Finland’s vibrant capital.

The application deadline is 15 February 2026 (Early bird, € 790).

Key Highlights:

  • Course Topic: Artificial Intelligence in Business
  • Location: Helsinki, Finland
  • Dates: 1–12 June 2026 (with a pre-assignment starting two weeks prior)
  • Target Group: Bachelor’s students (no prior AI or business background required)
  • Fees: Early Bird (until 15 Feb): € 790, Standard (until 15 Apr): € 940, Late (until 15 May): € 1090
    (Includes tuition, lectures, certificate, and one cultural activity)

Students will benefit from expert-led sessions, modern campus facilities, and the chance to build global connections in a dynamic learning environment.

Interested? Download the Fact Sheet about the Summer School Artificial Intelligence in Business right now.

More details and registration are available at the Summer School Website of Haaga-Helia.

Any questions? Please contact summerschool@haaga-helia.fi.

Agrobiodiversity in Galicia in September 2026

ZHAW Zürcher Hochschule für Angewandte Wissenschaften and partners offers a Summer School in september 2026, the topic is Agrobiodiversity. The Summer School is mainly directed to students enrolled in a Master program. Bachelor students are also invited to apply, but will be given second priority. The number of participants is limited to 30.

  • Dates: 2 to 12 September 2026 plus a preparatory phase in April and September
  • Location: Galicia (Spain)
  • Courses: sustainability and resilience of food systems, local production systems and food supply chains, 6 ECTS
  • Fee: CHF 250,- (app. EUR 270,-, plus travel costs)
  • Application deadline: Saturday, 28 February 2026

Further Information at a glance: Agrobiodiversity Summer School 2026, ZHAW

Contact: ZHAW, Dr. Roman Grüter und Hanna Della Casa.

Summer Schools 2026 in Madrid

Four Summer Schools offers the CUNEF Universität in Madrid 2026, all taught in Englisch. The 2 weeks courses start at different dates. Detailed information to all Summer Schools online:

  1. Fintech and the Future of Banking and Finance (6 ECTS)
  2. Artificial Intelligence (6 ECTS)
  3. Cybersecurity and Technological Risk (6 ECTS)
  4. Investments and Financial Markets (6 ECTS)
  • Dates: depending on the programme, starting from the 15th of June 2026
  • Location: Madrid
  • Fees: 1.200 Euro per programme. Students from partner universities (such as HTW Berlin) get a 10 % discount (1.080 Euro per programme).
  • Application deadline: end of April 2026

Further information at a glance is given in the CUNEF Universidad Summer School 2026 broschure (PDF).

Contact: summer@cunef.edu 

Summer Academy in Sweden in 2026

Linköping University (LiU) in Sweden invites you to a 4 weeks Summer Academy. The courses are at bachelor-level and in English. 5 students of partner universities like HTW Berlin can participate without a tuition fee.

  • Dates: 26 June to 25 July 2026
  • Site: Linköping, Sweden
  • Fees: no tuition fee (up to 5 students of HTW Berlin), accomodation 7.200 SEK  (approx. 660,- Euro)
  • Courses: Alternative Energy, Nanotechnology and others (7,5 ECTS each)
  • Application Period: 2 February to 20 March 2026

All information you need at a glance: Download the flyer of the LiU, now. Join Linköping University next summer!

Summer Schools in four cities 2026

7 English-taught programmes in 4 cities offers ESSCA Paris in 2026. They are open to undergraduate and postgraduate students. All programmes offer cultural visits, company tours, and social events. 

  • Dates: Juni bis Juli 2026
  • Site and Courses and Credits: 
  1. Malaga (2 programs) – Video Games / Global Management & Finance, 12 ECTS each
  2. Luxembourg (1 program) – Sustainable Finance & Blockchain, 12 ECTS each
  3. Budapest (2 programs) – Marketing & Leadership / EU-Asia Business, 12 ECTS each
  4. Paris (2 programs) – Luxury Branding / Global Leadership, 12 ECTS each
  • Tuition: depending on the Site of the Summer School, between 1.500 Euros (Budapest, excluding accomodation) and 5.000 Euro (Paris, including accomodation)
  • Application deadline: 1 April 2026

 Further inormation at a glance: Summer Schools 2026 ESSCA Paris

Septemberschool in Kapstadt 2026

DHIK Universities offer next year again a Septemberschool in South Africa. Subject: Sustainable Development: A holistic Approach. 

  • Dates: 30th of August to 12th of September 2026
  • Site: Cape Town, South Africa
  • Fees: 1.200 Euro (including accomodation and programme, excluding flight and travel expenses)
  • Courses: Sustainability & AI / IT, Renewable Energy, Global Sustainability Actions and others (2 ECTS each) 
  • Application deadline: 12th of April 2026

Interested? Further information provides the Hochschule Esslingen.

Want to apply directly? Go to the application form!

Summer School in India

The Indo-German Center For Higher Education, IGCHE, offers a Summer School in India in English in September.  

  • Dates: 6-9 September 2026
  • Site: PSG Institute of Management in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India
  • Fees: 1.200 Euro (including accomodation and programme, excluding flight and travel expenses like Visa)
  • Courses: Exploring Emerging Technologies in Business, Essentials of Business Management, Entrepreneurship Essentials und Understanding India's Economy, up to 3 ECTS 
  • Application deadline: 31 May 2026

Interested? Further information provides IGCHE on their website.

Any questions? Contact: contact@igche.de
