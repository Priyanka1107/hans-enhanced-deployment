Stay in touch!

There are many ways to stay in touch with HTW Berlin. By attending a Career Services course or passing on your practical knowledge to the next generation of students during a teaching assignment, for instance.

Alumni

Register to the HTW Berlin Alumni Network!

You can get advice on embarking on a career and starting a business even after graduation. Our Career Services are available to you up to a year after the end of your studies, while you can access the services of the Startup Competence Centre for five years after graduating.

Associate lecturers are a key pillar of teaching activities at HTW Berlin. Around 800 people offer courses on a wide variety of topics on a fee basis. If you would also like to pass on your knowledge to students, current vacancies for lecturer (in German) are published on the HTW Berlin website.

Pass on your professional experience to students! Either at the PraxisTag (in German), where a student accompanies you for a working day. You can also give students an insight into your work at an event organised by the Career Services or the Entrepreneurship team.

In addition, you can use your contacts at HTW Berlin to recruit young professionals for your company. You can publish your job offers in the vacancies section of the Career and Alumni Portal.

Your donation to HTW Berlin (in German) actively supports science and academe.

HTW Berlin is regularly on the search for reinforcements. Find out about the advantages of being employed at our university on the website "Working at HTW Berlin!". Current vacancies are advertised in the vacancies section.

Companies can get involved in the Deutschlandstipendium programme by funding a scholarship or a scholarship package for students.

Become a member of the HTW Berlin Friends Association (in German): Your membership fee supports the provision of material resources for degree programmes and the graduate prize awarded to the top-performing graduates in HTW Berlin’s five faculties. Personal involvement is also possible - by organising lecture events and excursions or in terms of networking.

Please contact the Student Service Centre at HTW Berlin for certificates, replacement copies and certified copies of your transcripts. Most services are subject to a fee.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
