Advice for prospective & current students

We support prospective students by providing information and advice on choosing a course of study, financing their studies and the application process. We advise students on how to organise their studies and discuss strategies they can use to complete their degrees successfully.

General Student Advisory Service

Dear applicants, please do not ask when you get an answer regaring your application and refrain from inquiring about your application status. Please be patient - we know it's hard! If you have other questions or problems regarding your application or enrolment please contact the Student Service Centre. Please give our colleagues about 2 weeks to reply. The General Student Advisory Service does not have access to your data. Thank you!

  • FAQ Application and application status on hochschulstart.de (in German)
  • Admission or refusal
  • Enrolment and Health insurance (Health insurance is mandatory for students in Germany. A health insurance has to report your insurance status. Hence, it's not something you can report to HTW Berlin.)

Personal consultations (no appointment necessary)

We don't make appointments. We have open consultation hours instead.

Treskowallee campus, building A

  • Thursdays: 2 p.m. - 5 p.m. (room 151)

Wilhelminenhof campus, building B

  • Tuesdays: 2 p.m. - 5 p.m. (room 004)
  • Wednesdays: 10 a.m. - 12 p.m. (room 004)

Please check our German website version for changes regarding our availability.

Telephone consultations

We answer brief questions and are available for longer consultation sessions on (030) 5019-2254:

  • Mondays and Fridays: 11 a.m. - 1 p.m.
  • Wednesdays: 2 p.m. - 4 p.m.
  • On Wednesdays and Fridays, questions can also be answered in English.
  • Please check our German website version for changes regarding our availability.

If the answering machine comes on during our office hours, it means that we are already engaged in a consultation session. Please try again later on.

Email

Email address: studienberatung@htw-berlin.de
Please provide your telephone number in case of any queries.
HTW students use their HTW email address.
Your email will be processed in a ticket system. As a result, please note the information on data protection.

Video consultations

Our video consultations take place by appointment. Please contact us in advance stating your specific request by emailing studienberatung@htw-berlin.de. We will then send you an email with the time slot and the access data.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
