Bachelor

Step 1:
Please register on the Hochschulstart website if you are applying for your first semester and your preferred degree programme is subject to a numerus clausus requirement.

List of NC degree programmes

At HTW Berlin, the NC requirement applies to the following Bachelor’s degree programmes:

  • Angewandte Informatik
  • Bauingenieurwesen
  • Bekleidungstechnik/Konfektion
  • Betriebswirtschaftslehre
  • Computer Engineering
  • Cyber Security and Business
  • Fahrzeugtechnik
  • Immobilienwirtschaft
  • Informatik und Wirtschaft
  • Ingenieurinformatik
  • International Business
  • Internationaler Studiengang Medieninformatik
  • Maschinenbau
  • Wirtschaft und Politik
  • Wirtschaftsinformatik
  • Wirtschaftsingenieurwesen
  • Wirtschaftskommunikation
  • Wirtschaftsrecht
✗ Hochschulstart

Are you already registered with Hochschulstart? Now all you need to do is apply for a study place! To do this, you will need your Hochschulstart access data (BID and BAN).

Step 2:

You should apply via the HTW Berlin application portal if one of the following requirements applies to you:

  • You have a German higher education entrance qualification.
  • You are a national of an EU or EEA country.
  • You are changing your degree programme within HTW Berlin.
  • You have successfully completed a preparatory course (Studienkolleg) in Germany.
✗ HTW application portal

The following application periods apply:

  • Winter semester: 15.05. - 15.07.
  • Summer semester: 15.11. - 15.01.

or

You should apply via uni-assist if you

  • have a foreign higher education entrance qualification and
  • are not a national of an EU or EEA country.
✗ uni-assist

The following application periods apply:

  • Winter semester: 02.04. - 30.06.
  • Summer semester: 01.10. - 31.12.

More information on the

  • Bachelor’s application via the HTW application portal
  • Bachelor’s application via uni-assist

Apply directly via the HTW application portal (without prior Hochschulstart registration) if you meet one of the following requirements:

Your preferred degree programme is not subject to the NC requirement.

At HTW Berlin, this applies to the following Bachelor’s degree programmes:

  • Betriebswirtschaftslehre - Fernstudium
  • Elektrotechnik
  • Gebäudeenergie- und -informationstechnik
  • Gesundheitselektronik
  • Informatik in Kultur und Gesundheit
  • Informations- und Kommunikationstechnik
  • Life Science Engineering
  • Maschinenbau - Fernstudium
  • Mikrosystemtechnik
  • Museologie
  • Regenerative Energien
  • Umweltinformatik
  • Wirtschaftsmathematik
  • Workplace und Facility Engineering
You have passed an aptitude test for a design- or culture-related degree programme.

At HTW Berlin, this applies to the following Bachelor’s degree programmes:

  • Game Design
  • Industrial Design
  • Kommunikationsdesign
  • Konservierung und Restaurierung/Grabungstechnik
  • Modedesign
You are applying for the O ja! orientation year training programme.
You are applying for your 2nd semester or a higher semester.
✗ HTW application portal

Application periods for the design- and culture-related degree programmes with aptitude tests:

  • Winter semester: 15.05. - 30.09.
  • Summer semester: 15.11. - 31.03.

Application periods for degree programmes not subject to the NC requirement:

  • Winter semester: 15.05. - 15.07.
    (Any free study places will be extended on an ongoing basis until max. 30.09.)
  • Summer semester: 15.11. - 15.01.
    (Any free study places will be extended on an ongoing basis until max. 30.03.)

Application period for the O ja! orientation year:

  • Summer semester: 15.11. - 15.01.

Application periods from the 2nd semester onwards:

  • Winter semester: 15.05. - 15.07.
  • Summer semester: 15.11. - 15.01.

More information on the

  • Bachelor’s application via the HTW application portal

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
