Academic self-administration at HTW Berlin

Academic self-administration is a fundamental principle governing universities in Germany - and therefore also an important aspect at HTW Berlin. As a professor, this enables you to play an active role in shaping the university, make decisions and take responsibility for central processes and structures.

Academic self-administration

As a professor at HTW Berlin, you can help shape the university in various committees, from the Faculty Council to appointments committees and the Academic Senate. Among other things, these decide on study regulations, appointments, research strategies and the further development of the university.

One focus of self-administration is the further development of teaching. As a professor, you will contribute to the practical and contemporary design of degree programmes.

In academic self-administration, you can actively influence the future of the university. You not only shape your own department, but also influence the strategic direction and profile of HTW Berlin as a whole.
