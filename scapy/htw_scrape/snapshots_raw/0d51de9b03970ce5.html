Why study at HTW Berlin?

  • Small groups: There are no large lectures at HTW Berlin. Learning takes place in groups with a maximum of 40 students.
  • Practical relevance: Modern laboratories, an internship semester in every bachelor's programme and lecturers with professional experience ensure practical relevance. 
  • Lots of support: From maths bridge courses before your studies to career advice after graduation: at HTW Berlin you will receive all-round support.
  • Good infrastructure: WLAN, computer pools and a well-equipped library offer the best study conditions.
  • International campus: Language courses in eight foreign languages, semesters abroad at 150 partner universities and English-language degree programmes open up international perspectives.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
