Focus on: new solar cells

Prof. Dr Bert Stegemann and his project team are working on ultra-thin and particularly efficient thin-film solar cells in cooperation with European partners. 

Research in the fight against serious diseases

Prof. Dr Hans Henning von Horsten is researching biopharmaceuticals that seek to provide new treatment options for serious diseases such as cancer, rheumatism and multiple sclerosis.

Economist with a penchant for history

Prof. Dr Regine Buchheim is an expert in international and external accounting whose research interests also include German history.

AI in corporate use

Prof. Dr Christina Kratsch is a computer scientist. Her research focuses on the question of how data and digital solutions can be used to generate sustainable added value in a company.

So much depends on exhibition graphics

Prof. Dr Jona Piehl focuses on the impact of exhibition graphics. She believes that this research area receives far too little attention.

Research

HTW Berlin cultivates a broad understanding of transfer and cooperates with numerous institutions and key players in the areas of science, business, management and culture. The Cooperation Centre for Applied Sciences acts as the central point of contact for cooperation enquiries, putting interested parties in touch with appropriate experts at HTW Berlin and helping to set up cooperative research activities.

Researchers at HTW Berlin follow an interdisciplinary and application-oriented approach to research and development with a strong relation to practice. The university’s research profile is shaped by five core future topics, forming the focal basis for its research clusters, research groups and institutions.

About HTW Berlin

HTW Berlin offers study, research and further education in the fields of business, engineering, computer science, design, culture, health, energy & environment, law, construction & real estate.

Enrol on one of the University’s 80 degree programmes - Bachelor, Master, MBA. Do research in scientific projects. Or attend the University’s further education courses.
